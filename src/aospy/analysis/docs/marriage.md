# Couche 3 — mariage châssis × aptitude

Détail déplacé de `analysis/README.md` (lire seulement avant de toucher ce module).

**Chassis × ability marriage** (`analysis/layers/marriage.py`, table `ability_marriage`, `aospy encounter marriage`, user's reframing): a good ability can be badly married (Oath of Supremacy on a 5"/5-HP Darkoath Warqueen). Each ability has a functional **family** (`abilities/families.py`, `ability.family`/`ability_effect.family`: buff-perso-offensif/defensif, buff-donne, debuff, degats-directs, mobilite, soin, controle, magie-ressource, autre). Marriage = **D × S**: D = P(the *bearer* meets its own conditions — charged via 2D6 ≥ gap − move, gap = 18" in round 1 then 14" (`ENGAGE_GAP_ROUND1`/`ENGAGE_GAP`), enemy territory reached after 9" + footprint (`TERRITORY_GAP`) — distances per season (`SEASON_GEOMETRY`, they change with each General's Handbook) — 2026-27 checked on its 12 battleplans: territories always touch, units deploy more than 9" from enemy territory (Deploy Unit, Core Rules p. 13), on objective, in combat, not moved/not charged) × share of rounds still possible; S = share of rounds the bearer survives to hold the effect, at the exposure its range imposes (contact ≤3", short ≤9", long ≥10" behind a screen) under staged pressure — 1 average enemy unit in round 1, 2 in rounds 2-3, 1 in rounds 4-5, damage accumulating (`ATTACKERS_PER_ROUND`, user's choice) — from `unit_benchmark` riposte split melee/ranged (estimated from effective wounds when absent); a « charged » bonus counts for half of a unit's fights (`CHARGE_FIGHT_SHARE`, user's choice). Conditions held by others (ally → layer 4, opponent → matchups, resource) are not priced, only listed. `unit_realized.*_fit*` (« ch. » columns of `encounter best`) = realized + bearer-held conditional effects × D. Every constant in `marriage.py` is a working hypothesis to challenge. **Per-family grid** (2026-11-21, user's « en avant » on the draft; buffs first): marriage = D × S × **F** × **A** × **R**, `ability_marriage.bearing` keeps D × S alone. D, S, F are probabilities/shares; A and R are bounded multipliers (`FACTOR_BOUNDS` 0.5-1.5: « better / worse than usual », one weak factor doesn't kill the marriage). **F tempo** (`tempo`, `effect_timing`): does the effect's window fall when *this* chassis acts? Relative at equal frequency (frequency itself is already weighted in layer 1 / `realized`): timeline coverage with the written duration ÷ the same with « until your next turn » — a melee buff « for the rest of the turn » covers half the fights of a unit that also fights back in the enemy turn (F = 0.5), a ward played in my turn covers 0.3 (`DEF_SHARE_ENEMY_TURN`); polarity and melee share read from the effects (ranged weapons → my turn only). **A amplification** (`amplification_ratios`): gain of the ability on this bearer (`ability_encounter`, relation « porteur ») ÷ the median positive gain of the same effect (kind, stat, value, weapon_scope) over every measured chassis (≥ `MIN_REFERENCE_SAMPLES`); the same effect varies 5-10× between chassis (+1 Attack melee: decile 1 = 6.7 %, median 24.6 %, decile 9 = 61.7 %); untranslated effects stay neutral. **R role** (`role_fit`): buff-perso-offensif 0.5 + offensive rank (A is relative, R gives the absolute weight: +100 % of a small number stays small); buff-perso-defensif 0.5 + mean(defensive rank, melee share); buff-donne « does the bearer keep up with its receivers »: min(1, bearer reach ÷ median receivers' reach) — Bring Every Gun to Bear! on the 7.5" Arkanaut Admiral vs 18.5" ships = 0.5. **Debuffs** (2026-11-22, user's answers, all three as proposed): D × `p_in_combat` (0.8 melee / 0.3 shooter, the buffs' « au combat » constants) when the target must be « in combat with this unit » (range ≤ 3") and no charge/combat/shot condition already says how the bearer gets there; F = the buffs' tempo rule with the polarity read from the debuff effects (`debuff_polarity`: weakening enemy attacks — hit/wound/attacks/damage/rend, Strike-last, no crit, no weapon abilities — is « def », 70 % of the hits in the enemy turn; weakening enemy defence — save, ward, no ward — is « off »; commands/control/magic/movement stay F = 1): Whispers of Desire, cast in my turn « for the rest of the turn », covers only the fight-back (F = 0.3); R (`debuff_role_fit`) at contact only: « def » → 0.5 + defensive rank (the bearer is the one being hit: Roar of Domination on Abraxia 1.5, on the Lord Executioner 0.6), « off » → 0.5 + offensive rank (its own blows profit: Raging Inferno on the Magmadroth Runefather 1.27), ranged debuffs 1 (the beneficiary is picked in the list, layer 4); A neutral (debuffs are not translated by the engine). Exposure: a debuff set by the bearer's own shooting (requirement `après tir du porteur`, analyser v35) is held at its longest ranged weapon's range, not at contact; an army-wide debuff never imposes contact. **Direct damage** (2026-11-22, user's answers, all as proposed): D × `p_in_combat` for « an enemy unit in combat with this unit » (same as debuffs); a target to fly over (`a survolé la cible`) → `p_overfly`, the share of rounds where move (+ fly) + 3.5" run + 3" target depth ≥ the gap (18" in round 1, then 14": Ironclad 1.0, Hellflayer 0.89); retaliation (`riposte au contact`, analyser v35: mortal damage on the attacker when the bearer is hit in combat — save of 6, model slain, damage point allocated) → contact, D × `p_in_combat`, R = 0.5 + defensive rank (an anvil retaliates longer: Blood of Molten Varanite on Abraxia 1.5); mortal damage riding on its own attacks (`sur ses attaques`: Cometstrike Sceptre, Outrageous Carnage) → judged like a personal offensive buff (exposure from the melee share, R = 0.5 + offensive rank); F and A neutral (instant effects). **Mobility** (2026-11-23, user's answers, all four as proposed): a *personal* mobility (scope self, or an enemy target as destination: Drawn By Blood) takes the exposure of a personal buff (contact for a melee unit, `longue` for a shooter) and is split by `mobility_kind` into engagement (charge aids — `stat_mod`/`dice_count`/`reroll` on charge, run-and-charge = +3.5", pile-in, « must end that move in combat », « this unit has charged »), disengagement (retreat without mortal damage, run-and-shoot, « use the 'Retreat' ability », « cannot end that move in combat », a move or teleport after charging / after slaying: Strike and Fade, Sudden Translocation) and repositioning (reserve, teleport, move bonus, fly — the rest). R (`mobility_role_fit`): engagement/repositioning 0.5 + offensive rank (« what the chassis does on arrival »: Hidden Loons on Loonsmasha Fanatics 1.5, A Twisted Fate on a Chaos Spawn 0.53); disengagement 0.5 + max(offensive rank, 1 − defensive rank) (it charges again, or it survives: Sneaky Little Devils on Chaos Furies 1.46). A (`mobility_amplification`, « a slow chassis gains more »): a charge aid = P(charge) gained on this chassis (`charge_gain`, exact dice via `assault.p_roll`, gaps 18" then 14") ÷ the median gain of the bearers of charge aids (`bearer_charge_reference`, +12 points in 2026-27: Inescapable Wrath on Skarbrand 1.5, Heralds of Righteousness on 12"-flying Prosecutors 0.5); otherwise median chassis reach (8", move + fly) ÷ bearer reach (Master of the Slime Fleet on 4" Gutrot Spume 1.5); disengagement neutral. A *given* mobility (aura, order, teleporting a friendly unit) uses the buff-donne rule, the bearer keeping up with its receivers — receivers re-read with `encounter.receivers` (`mobility_receiver_reach`, mobilities are not translated by the engine) — except deployment-phase reserves; A neutral. F neutral (instant). Analyser v36 also fixed `trigger`: « au combat » is free only for an effect on melee weapons; with no weapon scope (Share The Pain, « If this unit is in combat, it can move 3" ») it now costs P(au combat) (all() of an empty list was true). Effect on the family: mean marriage 0.76 → 0.49 (130 down, 24 up of 222), mostly from the contact exposure; known limit: a caster with no ranged weapon has `share_melee` 1 and is exposed at contact (The Four Corners of the Realms on Erasmus Zonn 0.07). **Healing** (2026-11-23, user's answers, all four as proposed): a heal on the bearer itself (`_self_heal`) takes the exposure of a personal buff (contact for a melee unit); A = rounds of survival the heal adds (`heal_survival_gain`: the same staged pressure as S, healing after each round survived, never more than the damage taken; « each turn » = 2 uses per round, its own turn 1, once per battle once in round 2) ÷ the median gain of self-heals on their bearers (`heal_reference`); points per use follow the healing rule (`heal_points`: nothing on 1-HP models, capped at Health − 1 on a multi-model unit; a model return is worth N × Health, nothing on a single model; « Heal (X) » unmeasured → neutral): Regeneration on Rockgut Troggoths 1.5, on Fellwater Troggoths 0.52; R = the personal defensive buff rule (0.5 + mean(defensive rank, melee share): Dead for Innumerable Ages on Lord Kroak 1.27, Regenerating Terror on the Brood Terror 0.83). A heal triggered by its own fighting (« by this unit's combat attacks », « each time this unit uses a FIGHT ability », analyser v36 requirement `au combat`) pays P(au combat) — a melee weapon scope no longer makes it free — and a specific enemy to destroy (« that enemy HERO was destroyed », `target_condition` « élimine un HERO ») is a listed good case, not priced. A *given* heal or Rally bonus uses the buff-donne rule (receivers re-read with `encounter.receivers`, `mobility_receiver_reach(family="soin")`), A neutral; healing a friendly unit « within this unit's combat range » (Mobile Repairs) is `courte`, not contact. F neutral. Effect on the family: mean marriage 0.82 → 0.50 (41 down, 7 up of 61), mostly from the contact exposure. **Control** (2026-11-23, user's answers: exposure « always at contact » instead of my personal-buff proposal, the other three as proposed): a control bonus on the bearer itself (`_personal_control`: not a friendly scope, not `battlefield` — « You control the target objective » is held by no unit) is exposed at contact (a held objective always gets hit); A = P(beating a random warscroll's control score on an objective, ties half) gained ÷ the median gain of personal control bonuses (`ControlReference`, models × Control of every warscroll, median 5: Zealous Acolytes on 3 Anointed Sentinels 44 % → 98 % = 1.5, His Will Be Done on 6 Questor Soulsworn 79 % → 83 % = 0.5); R = 0.5 + mean(rank of its base control score, defensive rank) (`control_role_fit`: holding = numbers and staying power). A *given* control bonus uses the buff-donne rule (`mobility_receiver_reach(family="controle")`), A neutral. Enemy control reduced/capped (analyser v36) is family `debuff`, graded by the debuff grid. F neutral. Effect: 25 of 53 pairs left the family (20 Beast drawbacks → `autre`, 5 → `debuff`); the 28 left go 0.67 → 0.48 (13 down, 2 up), mostly from the contact exposure. Known limit: « Add X » with X = a faction resource (Fiery Disposition, X = fury level) reads 1. **Magic and resources** (2026-11-23, user's answers, all three as proposed; analyser v37): a casting/chanting/unbinding/power-level bonus on the bearer itself (`stat_mod`/`dice_count`/`reroll` on cast, chant, unbind, banish, power_level) → R = 0.5 + 0.25 × caster level (`magic_role_fit`, `caster_level` reads « WIZARD (N) » / « PRIEST (N) »: the level is how many spells/prayers the bonus serves per turn — Regalia Fulmentarus on WIZARD (2) Krondys 1.0, The Staff of Power on Nagash 1.5, a casting bonus on a priest 0.5), A neutral (casting values are 6-8 almost everywhere and the lore is chosen in the list, layer 4); a *given* bonus (auras, free commands) uses the buff-donne rule (`mobility_receiver_reach(family="magie-ressource")`); anti-magic, faction resources and replacement units are neutral (their value depends on the opponent, the list or the resource). F neutral. Effect: 16 of 138 pairs left the family (9 set-ups from reserve → `mobilite`, 7 Battle Damaged → `autre`); the 122 left go 0.84 → 0.82 (8 up, 37 down). Every family now has its grid. Tooling: `scratch/marriage_compare.py` (alias `tm-aospy-mariages`) — `check [--family X]` replays `ability analyze` + `encounter build` on a copy of the DB and prints the rises/falls, `dump X` lists a family's pairs (text, effects, bearers), `snap`/`diff` compare two states. Enhancement bearers (`bearers.py`) value = gain × coverage × D × S only (the measured gain and the timeline already hold A and F); their `marriage` column carries F × R too. Known quirk (pre-existing, not changed): a once-per-battle short effect is assumed played in round 2, so a fragile contact bearer that dies in round 1 gets D × S = 0 (7 pairs, e.g. Battle-fury / Grimwrath Berzerker). Layer 3 judges whether a *bearer* carries a support ability well; whether anyone benefits is layer 4 (user's banner example). **Good bearers of enhancements** (`analysis/layers/bearers.py`, table `enhancement_bearer`, `aospy encounter bearers --ability X | --unit X | --army X`): every heroic trait/artefact/enhancement/monstrous trait × every eligible unit of its faction, ranked (user: « les bons porteurs », a list, not one winner). Eligibility = `ability.bearer_restriction`, read from the Wahapedia group/type description (`source.bearer_restriction_of`: « HERO only », « Non-OGOR IRONSUNZ HERO only », « … can be given to non-Hero X Infantry or Cavalry units », named warscrolls, « with the STANDARD BEARER keyword », « excluding … »; a stray « non- HERO » space is normalised), default HERO (HERO MONSTER for monstrous traits); UNIQUE units never (Core Rules p. 37). Value = absolute gain of √(damage × durability) on that bearer (enhancements cost no points) × coverage × marriage; enhancements with no translatable effect (bubbles, spells…) are ranked by marriage then by the bearer's combined indicator. Several abilities share a name across groups (artefact vs Army-of-Renown enhancement): listed separately.
