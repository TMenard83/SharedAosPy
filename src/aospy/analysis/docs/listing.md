# Couche 4 — aptitudes × armée

Détail déplacé de `analysis/README.md` (lire seulement avant de toucher ce module).

4. **Abilities × army** (`analysis/layers/listing.py`, `aospy roster analyze --list <file> [--out x.md]`, Markdown via `cli/report.format_list_report`): the *numbers* a human list analysis builds on — never the analysis itself (concept, roles, round-by-round plan stay hand-written, user's method in memory `list-building-concept`). Per unit: layer-3 values and centile ranks, a suggested role (hammer / glass cannon / anvil / caster / support / specialist when the good case ≥ 1.3 × realized), earliest engagement round, abilities badly married to their bearer (`ability_marriage` < 0.4); enhancements taken vs the best eligible bearer *in the list* (`enhancement_bearer`); dead rules (prayer/spell lore with no priest/wizard, friendly-target abilities with no receiver in the list, and — user's banner example — **buffs nobody in the list profits from**: `unprofitable_buffs`, a friendly-scope ability whose translatable effects are all worth 0 on every list receiver, e.g. « +1 to hit for ranged attacks » with no shooter; abilities with untranslated effects or an opponent-dependent gain (Anti-X, « vs HERO ») are not judged); **army rules picked from the list's origin**: every base battle-trait subtype (« Battle Traits › * »: Khorne's « Blood Tithe Abilities », Idoneth's « Battle Traits1 » were missed before), the chosen formation and lores — and, for an **Army of Renown**, each category it carries *replaces* the base one (`renown_categories`, same rule as the roster PDF's `rules_resolution._AOR_REPLACES`; a category it lacks, the formation, stays base). The AoR is read from « Faction | AoR » (`ParsedList.army_of_renown`) or, in the compact export, from the formation slot — unless a base formation has that very name (Sylvaneth « Lords of the Clan » is both: the formation wins). A named enhancement is looked up in the list's AoR first, then in base rules — never in another AoR, a Spearhead army or Path to Glory; list effects × list receivers weighted on the time axis × trigger D (a « charged » condition limits the effect to my turn — the time axis then halves it for a melee unit, no separate charge share); army-wide limits; magic budget; abilities of the manifestations the chosen lore can summon (`ListAbility.summon_prob` = P of the SUMMON spell: a source posed by a manifestation only exists if the summon passes). **Assault** (`analysis/layers/assault.py`, calibrated on the user's own Hibou_2 analysis: a grouped-charge plan holds by *redundancy* and *fallbacks*, not by one charge's reliability): chargers = the list's melee hitters (`share_melee` ≥ 0.5, melee damage ≥ ½ of the best), charge bonuses (`ability_effect.stat = 'charge'`) and full charge rerolls (`reroll` with `stat = 'charge'`, analyser v25; a partial one-die reroll isn't counted) — receivers from the effect's own `ability_effect.friendly_target` (v26), else the target the sentence writes, else the ability's, dice-count effects (`dice_count`, analysers v28-v29: 3D6 summed, 3 dice keep the best 2, lowest D6 set to the highest — exact probabilities, a 9" charge is 74 % / 52 % / 56 % vs 28 % on 2D6; the best available mode is taken per distance, their order changes with it), the generic reroll command (1 CP, one charge per turn, rerolls the most promising failure); exact P(≥ k successes of N) after a teleport (> 9" → a 9 suffices); on foot, a charge is not a plan but a punishment of the opponent's mistakes (user), so it is shown as a **threat range** per charger (`Threat`: move + longest charge made at ≥ 50 %, dry / with bonuses / with reroll); with the summon probability and the teleport spell's success folded in. Not counted: enemy unbinding, second teleport attempts, casting order (stays hand-written). **Layer-3 grids replayed on the list** (`analysis/layers/listgrid.py`, 2026-11-23, user's choice « chiffrer avec la liste » before combos): every ability the engine does not translate (the former « à lire » list) gets a row — the bearer's layer-3 marriage (`ability_marriage`, or `enhancement_bearer` for an enhancement), the **keep-up** against the list's *actual* receivers (`keep_up`: bearer reach ÷ median reach of its list receivers, capped at 1, the validated buff-donne rule) and what each list receiver gets, measured with its family's grid: heal → survival rounds gained (`marriage.heal_survival_gain`, at the receiver's own personal exposure; « nothing » on 1-HP models or single models for returns), control → objective won before → after (`marriage.ControlReference`), casting/chanting bonus → spells/prayers served per turn (receiver's WIZARD/PRIEST level), debuff → who in the list profits (« off »: its hitters, damage ≥ ½ of the best; « def »: its contact units), mobility → its sub-kind (`marriage.mobility_kind`; charge aids and charge rerolls stay in « Assaut »), direct damage → target-dependent. Drawbacks (`listgrid.is_drawback`: only `contrepartie`/`malus`/`auto-infligé` effects, Battle Damaged, restrictions on its own unit — The Pendulum Swings) leave the reading. Report section « Aptitudes non traduites, rejouées sur la liste ». **Stacking** (`analysis/layers/stacking.py`, first combo kind, user's choice): for each list unit, every translatable effect it can receive plus the core commands All-out Attack (+1 to hit, Companion included; its −1 save for the rest of the turn is left out — the durability case assumes All-out Defence in the enemy turn) and All-out Defence (+1 save), their CP cost read from the handbook verbatim (`data/json/generic_core_abilities.json`, user's choice « avec le budget PC »), applied *together*: the ±1 cap bears on the *total* of hit/wound/save modifiers (`_cap`), one option per exclusive-choice ability (the best on that unit), wards don't stack; compared to the *product* of the effects taken alone (independent effects multiply: +1 Attack × +1 to hit); « plafonné » = adds nothing once the others are in (e.g. Claw-picked and The Thirteen-headed One on Lord Skreech, two +1 to hit next to All-out Attack). Sorted by absolute damage « all in place » (the list's Death-Star). A « plafonné » item says why (`StackItem.capped_reason`, 2026-11-23, lessons of Hibou_2/Haldahir): « plafond ±1 / non cumulable » is a true duplicate, « sauvegarde 4+ déjà impossible » adds against a 3+ save (extra Rend once the reference save can no longer drop — Claw-picked and The Thirteen-headed One on Lord Skreech); damage « all in place » is also given against a 3+ save. **Single-target choices** (`stacking.target_choices`, section « Aptitudes à une seule cible : sur qui les poser », user's lesson « croiser les règles », 2026-11-23): an ability that targets one friendly unit is ranked on each possible receiver by its *real* contribution with everything else that unit receives in place (`StackItem.off_marginal` = joint − joint without it) — Blood-drenched on Bloodletters that already have Commander of Tyrants' +1 Attack. Analyser v39 (found here): a « named » weapon that is a weapon ability (« its Blood-hungry weapons ») matches the weapons carrying it; « If the target is BLOODBOUND / a non-MONSTER DAEMON unit / a MONSTER, … » restricts that option line; « Ignore positive modifiers to save rolls for the target » on a friendly target is its drawback; « another visible friendly » excludes the bearer; and `matches_friendly_target` no longer lets « non-MONSTER » swallow the next keyword (« non-MONSTER DAEMON » excluded every daemon). **Review of the 12 lists by agents (2026-11-23, analyser v40)**: layer-4 stacking is measured *charged* (« all in place »: Charge (+1 Damage), Mindrazor); `teleporters` = the real receivers of reserve/teleport abilities (no more « any HERO when a lore can teleport »); dead rules: a bearer that is its own target (« pick this unit and up to 2 … »), a faction terrain or manifestation as target, a caster level gained by an ability (« it has WIZARD (1) », Broken Nadja), a keyword gained in play (pledges), and — newly dead — a spell/prayer/command that needs a user of an absent keyword (« Pick a friendly STORMCAST ETERNALS WIZARD to cast this spell »), an army rule with no receiver; a « doit avoir chargé » flag is no longer spread to every option when layer 1 already put it on one effect (Blessing of Zeal); two units of the same name with different sizes stay distinct (« … (renforcé) »); list points are shown. Analyser v40: « Companion weapons » = only the Companion weapons (`weapon_scope` « companion »: Itzl, Enraged Roar, Menagerie). `matches_friendly_target`: a faction keyword written in title case (« Kruleboyz ») is a keyword, « Shadow Queen » matches « The Shadow Queen ». The report warns at the top when list units are missing from the database (« chiffres faux sur une liste incomplète »), and `listing.find_unit` resolves the App's name variants (« Scourge of Aqshy: X », typographic apostrophe, faction prefix « Kruleboyz Monsta-killaz », repeated word « Kurnoth Hunters with Kurnoth Greatswords ») and reinforced units (models doubled). Not weighted by reliability: conditions are shown. **Giver reliability** (2026-11-23, user's answer « D × S × suivi »): a buff *given* by another list unit is weighted by its giver — the bearer's layer-3 D × S (`ability_marriage.bearing`) × min(1, giver reach ÷ receiver reach), × P(summon) for a manifestation (`listing.giver_reliability`, `Marriage.giver`, column « donneur » of « Mariages chiffrés »); own effects and army rules without a bearer weigh 1 **Givers recomputed with the list** (`analysis/layers/givers.py`, 2026-11-23, user's remark on Totosai's Scinari Enlightener — Silver Wand +1, Scholar of the Sigils on Thalari +1, the Shrine Luminor's reroll and « All attacks that would target the Shrine Guardian target this terrain feature instead »: « on ne peut pas appeler ça fragile »): the giver's D and S come from the list, not from the bare layer-3 bearer — casting = P(2D6 ≥ value − every casting bonus it receives in the list), with a reroll 1 − (1 − P)² (no ±1 cap on casting rolls: the core cap bears on hit, wound and save), an option fixed by an enhancement that « cannot be depicted » (Scholar's rune) being unavailable to every other ability; survival = the layer-3 pressure on its effective wounds grown by the protections it receives (stacking without commands) plus, for **one guardian per faction terrain** (the highest-level caster, then the frailest), the terrain's effective wounds (Wahapedia profile: Shrine Luminor 12 HP, 3+). Enlightener: casting 58 % → 97 %, survival 42 % → 100 %. These values replace layer 3 in the giver reliability, and a lore spell (no fixed caster) takes the best caster of the list (REALMSHIELD 0.58 → 0.97). Limit: an ability with exclusive options may use one option for casting (The Thirteen-headed One « Master of Dire Sorcery ») and another in the marriages (« Knower of Warp-tech ») — one per turn in play. **Missing providers** (`listing.missing_providers`): an ability whose text needs a friendly ally in range (« wholly within 12" of a friendly Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood, Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood or Shrine of Dark Tribute », « within the combat range of a friendly non-HERO SKAVEN INFANTRY unit », « If a friendly X is on the battlefield ») with no matching unit, faction terrain or manifestation in the list joins the dead rules — a composition fact; no real list triggers it today. **Chains** (`analysis/layers/chains.py`, section « Enchaînements »): *disengage → Rally* is measured (user's answer « survie gagnée, 1 fois par round »): a unit that can leave combat (a `mobilite` ability of sub-kind disengagement it carries or receives) takes one Rally per round (core command, 1 CP, out of combat only: 6 dice + musician + the list's « additional rally rolls », one point per 4+, a point heals 1 or a model costs its Health) — survival rounds gained (`marriage.heal_survival_gain`) × P(disengage); CP are not arbitrated between units. *Mobile shooting* (« If this unit used a SHOOT ability this phase, this unit can move ») and *range relays* (« measure range and visibility from either part of this MANIFESTATION », a faction-terrain guardian) are listed. Analyser v38 (found here): « friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY and CAVALRY units » is army-wide and `matches_friendly_target` distributes the prefix over coordinated keywords (SKAVEN INFANTRY or SKAVEN CAVALRY — requiring both matched nobody); « within 12" of any friendly X units » is a condition, not the receivers (Spawn of Sotek); « While a friendly X is wholly within …: That non-HERO unit can … » is army-wide unless it targets enemy units (ALL TO DUST); 9 102 → 9 066 encounters. **Build-up over time** (`analysis/layers/buildup.py`, section « Montée en puissance », user's answers « plan automatique » and « afficher la courbe »): the list's stacking (without commands) is replayed for each round 1-5 with what is available then — an ability from its `available_round` on (Blood Tithe), and a rune board (« Each rune remains on your battle scripture », DEPICT RUNE): one rune per round, its effect only for that round, but it stays on the board for the « for each instance of <rune> depicted » abilities, whose effect is multiplied by the copies (With the Force of a Landslide: +1 Attack per Alaithi); greedy plan (each round, the rune that adds most to the list's relative damage + durability given the board), minus a rune an enhancement takes (`givers.claimed_options`). Round weights stay 1/3, 1, 1, 1/3, 1/3; the curve and its peak are shown (« liste de fin de partie » when the last round is worth ≥ 15 % more). **Rôles et composantes** (`analysis/layers/roles.py`, 2026-11-25, section « Rôles dans la liste » de la fiche, calculée dans `format_list_report`) : six rôles de l'utilisateur (marteau, enclume, cordon, scoring, support, mobilité) + « ajustement », chacun scoré 0-1 à partir du profil (rangs centiles couche 3) ET de ce que l'unité reçoit (dégâts / PV effectifs « tout en place » de l'empilement, rapportés à la moyenne par unité de la liste) ; contrôle par 100 pts pour le scoring ; bulles données à d'autres receveurs (`rep.marriages`) + niveau de sorcier/prêtre pour le support ; mobilité = mouvement + VOL + **part de téléportation** (`teleport_share` : utilisations par tour — une par porteur, une par sorcier pour un sort de domaine — réparties sur les receveurs ; Transporting Vortex d'un seul Windmage sur 5 unités = 0,2 chacune, Skitterleap avec 6 sorciers Skaven = 1 ; « tout receveur d'une téléportation » saturait la mobilité de toutes les listes). Mobilité principale seulement si elle dépasse le reste de 0,15. Composantes : château lent (support lent dont les bulles tiennent ≥ 2 unités lentes), aile rapide (unités rapides marteau/mobilité hors château, ≥ 15 % des points ; rapide = M ≥ 10", ≥ 8" avec VOL, ou téléportée ≥ 0,5/tour — `territory_round` rendait toutes les unités « rapides »), deathstar (≥ 3 effets, dégâts ×1,8, ≥ 20 % des points, score marteau ≥ 0,6), écran (≥ 2 cordons). Seuils réglés à la main sur Hibou_2, Totosai, Cartoone, Haldahir, Razemme : suggestion, pas verdict.

**Forces et faiblesses** (`analysis/layers/strengths.py`, 2026-11-25, section « Forces et faiblesses (synthèse automatique) » de la fiche) : chaque faiblesse porte ce qui la punit, chaque force ce qui la neutralise (entrée de `match-analysis`). Mesures pondérées par les points : activations, contrôle/100 pts, part de tir, part rapide, concentration des dégâts « tout en place », sauvegarde 3+ sans ward / 5+ sans ward / ward 5+, perforation moyenne, mots-clés exposés aux Anti-X, Anti-X portés, part des mariages sous charge ou par sort, donneurs < 0,6, supports fragiles (rang dur < 0,3), montée en puissance. Seuils = quartiles des 54 listes L3 (médianes `REF` affichées en repère) ; `engage_round` écarté (R1 sur 54/54). Fréquences au calage : 1-5 forces, 0-8 faiblesses par liste ; « donneur peu fiable » ne sort jamais (seuil de score 5 à revoir si besoin).

**Grid readings as numbers** (2026-11-25, for the L3 indices): each `listgrid.GridRow` also carries `gains` (receiver, value: survival gained for a heal, control-score bonus for a control ability), the ability's `scope` and a `weight` = P(it serves) = layer-1 activation (or casting recomputed with the list, `listing._activation`) × P(summon) × keep-up; a once-per-battle control ability weighs 1/2. `l3.layer4.heal_and_control` turns them into list indices (heal → durability share, control → added control score).

**Lecture de l'export dans `collect`** (2026-11-25, contrôles des listes L3) : `find_entry_unit` lit le préfixe saisonnier (« Scourge of Aqshy: X » ; « Scourge of Ghyran: X », pack 2025-26 filtré à l'import → la variante Aqshy, même profil, sinon la charte de base) ; les membres d'un **Régiment de Renom** comptent (cherchés dans toutes les armées, aptitudes lues dans leur faction d'origine, points du régiment répartis au prorata du coût en base) ; un **décor de faction** est lu sans son coût (« Charnel Throne (10 Points) ») et une option qui porte le nom d'une charte sans profil de la faction (« • Stormreach Portal (20 pts) ») est un décor ; `TERRAIN_DEPLOYS` ajoute les unités déployées par un décor (Zontari Endrin Dock → Auto-Endrin) ; un **compagnon gratuit** omis de l'export (`companions.COMPANIONS` : Morathi-Khaine → The Shadow Queen…) est ajouté à 0 pt. La couche 3 (`realized.build_realized_rows`) chiffre ces compagnons sur leur part du coût du parent (prorata des PV), avec un rang lu dans le pool des unités payantes sans le déplacer.

**Contrôles des analyses de listes L3 (2026-11-25, analyseur v42)** — erreurs de lecture relevées par les 54 analyses (`scratch/output/liste_analyse/Contrôles analyses adverses 2026-11-25.txt`) :
- **Groupes exclusifs entre aptitudes** (`listing.EXCLUSIVE_KEYWORDS`, mot-clé d'aptitude Wahapedia `ability_keywords`, repli « While you believe this DELUSION ») : un seul Délire cru (FEC), un seul Astérisme (Seraphon ; les suivants de « Further the Great Plan » ne sont pas comptés). Par receveur, la meilleure aptitude du groupe dans les mariages (`_best_in_group`), l'empilement (7e champ des offres de `stack_for`) et l'assaut (`ChargeSource.group`). Limite : le choix est fait unité par unité, pas une fois pour l'armée.
- **Assaut** : paliers d'une même aptitude non additionnés (« Add 2 … instead », Defenders of the Kingdom) ; bonus d'une aptitude dont l'utilisateur doit avoir chargé (« Pick a friendly PARAGON that charged this turn », Chief of Revels) hors bonus permanents (`after_charge`) ; « cannot pick the same HERO … more than once per battle » n'est pas 1×/bataille (Waaagh!) ; charge-embuscade (« where X is equal to the charge roll … this unit has charged », Merciless Ambush) jugée à `AMBUSH_GAP` = 6" du décor au lieu de 9" ; portée de menace = mouvement + bonus de Mouvement passifs reçus (`move_bonuses` : War-horn, Harvestboon → Kurnoth à épées 10").
- **Règles mortes à tort** : désignation d'allié coupée avant « to use / to be the target » (Celestial Translocation, Roused to Fury) ; « a friendly SKYVESSEL in the clouds to use » (Descend from the Clouds) ; sort d'invocation dont la cible amie est dans la liste (Summon Dais Arcanum : `receivers` ignore les manifestations) ; cible facultative quand l'effet porte aussi sur le porteur (« You can pick … Remove this unit », Relictor Soulguide).
- **Receveurs** : règle d'armée « sur soi » qui nomme sa charte (« the friendly Morathi-Khaine », Dark Majesty) → cette charte seule (`_NAMED_FRIENDLY_RE`) ; mot-clé gagné une unité par tour (« PLEDGED TO … », Pledge to Chaos) → aptitude à cible unique (`target_choices`) ; indices L3 : `stacking.single_target_stacks` (second passage sans la cible secondaire) remplace le retrait de l'apport marginal, qui ne retirait rien quand deux protections se substituent.
- **Prières répétées** (`listing.prayer_turn_factor`) : par tour, max(P(D6 ≥ N), P stockée ÷ 2) au lieu de la P « au round clé » (Bless Weapons 64 % → 32 %, Fortifying Hoarfrost 78 % → 50 %) ; une prière 1×/bataille garde le round clé.
