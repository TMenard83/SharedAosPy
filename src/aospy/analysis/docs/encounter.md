# Couche 3 — rencontre aptitude × châssis, axe temporel

Détail déplacé de `analysis/README.md` (lire seulement avant de toucher ce module).

3. **Ability × chassis encounter** (`analysis/layers/encounter.py`, table `ability_encounter`, `aospy encounter build|list`): what a *friendly* effect really yields on *this* warscroll, via the engine — +1 Damage on 1-damage weapons is +100 %, on 3-damage +33 %; +N Attacks on a Companion weapon is nothing. Each effect becomes a **profile rewrite** (copy of the `Unit`, like `bonus_simulator.apply_bonus`, extended to the effect perimeter: melee/ranged/named weapon, `chosen` = best weapon, Companion excluded unless stated; hit/wound/save modifiers capped at ±1; crits/Anti-X/Charge appended to `Weapon.abilities` text, which `combat.py` already parses; wards don't stack) — `combat.py` untouched. Measured before/after with the chassis probes (round damage vs save 4+, effective wounds), stored as **conditional** gains (« if it applies »): reliability/duration/prerequisites stay as columns to weight later. Receivers: own warscroll for `self`, army units satisfying `ability.friendly_target` (analyser v6: the friendly-target restriction as written, « non-HERO WARRIORS OF CHAOS INFANTRY », resolved against unit keywords — a keyword no warscroll carries, e.g. one gained in play, doesn't filter — except a possessive Regiment-of-Renown keyword, « DROGGZ'S GITMOB », which matches no army unit); a « combat enchaîné » requirement (a buff for the unit that fights right after the hero: Brawlerguts, Lead Da Brutes…) is weighed by `marriage.CHAINED_FIGHT_SHARE` = 0.5 (user's choice) instead of being dropped from the support value for friendly scopes, heroes for traits/artefacts/enhancements; `source` keeps the owner group (formation, lore, Army of Renown…) since those only apply when chosen: « <category of that carrier>: <type › subtype> », « (armée de renom) » added for an AoR carrier; a (ability, effect, receiver) row keeps the base carrier before an AoR one. Spearhead and Path to Glory carriers are skipped (not matched play, `ability_owner.origin_kind`, analyser v32), here and in `bearers.py`. `fight_twice` (analyser v8: « can use 2 FIGHT abilities this phase/turn », e.g. Gunnar Brand every turn, Battle-fury once per battle) = melee output ×2. Not translated (v1): `extra_fight` (« can be picked to use a FIGHT ability immediately after », activation chaining: tempo, not output), enemy-side debuffs, rerolls (engine has none), « ignore the first damage point », manifestations. **Realized value / best units** (`analysis/layers/realized.py`, table `unit_realized`, `aospy encounter best`): chassis + the warscroll's *own* abilities that boost the unit itself (`self`, or a friendly bubble/order it is an eligible target of — `ability.excludes_self`, analyser v10, drops « another/other friendly »); heroic traits/artefacts/army rules/other units' buffs are list-level (layer 4). Weights validated by the user: rounds 2-3 weigh 1, rounds 1/4/5 weigh 1/3 (opening, mop-up, scoring); passive/permanent or reusable every turn/phase/round → 1 (« once per turn (army) » only prevents spamming across several units: list-level, not here); once per battle short effect → 1/2 (played at the kairos, the tipping point); once per battle lasting the rest of the battle → 8/9 (activated round 2); × `activation_prob` (prayers: `extract.p_prayer_banked`, ritual points banked in round 1 then chanted at the key round — Core Rules p. 34 — and prayers can't be unbound). Conditions (charged, vs HERO, on objective…) get no weight: they are interactions that orient the list, so they're excluded from `*_realized` and added in `*_good_case`. Offence is read through a **play plan** (`analysis/layers/approach.py`, `encounter.play_plan`, user request « modélise l'approche, la portée est un facteur important »): the 5 rounds are simulated from the season's 18" start gap — melee plan: move (fly included), shoot what is in range, charge (2D6 ≥ remaining gap) or run if nothing is possible, then stay in contact, shooting only « Shoot in Combat » guns; shooting plan per distinct weapon range L: stand at L" (round 1: no closer than start gap − move), shoot what reaches, charged each enemy turn with `ENEMY_CHARGE_SHARE` (0.5) × P(2D6 ≥ L − `ENEMY_MOVE` 6"), then stuck in combat. The enemy advances 6" per turn. A `Plan` = round types (share of the game weighted by `DEFAULT_RHYTHM`, set of weapons striking), each read at the `FLOOR_COVERAGE` floor; the bare chassis picks its best plan, effects are measured on that same plan. Effect on the ranking: pure melee −11 % offence (round 1 spent approaching), 18-24" shooters up, 10-12" shooters down. Hypotheses to challenge: those constants, I play first in round 1, a charged shooter never gets free. Per-family damage goes through `features.weapon_damages` (weapon counts resolved on the *whole* unit: measuring a weapon subset alone re-resolves « 1 of the following » choices per subset — Stormfiends read 21.5 instead of 14.2); the cost model's `dmg_melee_*`/`dmg_ranged_*` features and `dominant_crit_type` use it too (R² adj 0.879 → 0.880 vs per-subset resolution, Stormfiends' predicted cost 333 → 311). Ranking indicator (user-validated): `combined_per_100` = √(damage/100 × durability/100) (Lanchester logic: what a unit deals over its lifetime needs both — glass cannons such as Loonsmasha Fanatics no longer top it), ranked separately for troops and heroes (`combined_pct_rank`; heroes pay for support abilities outside this scope). Damage is read like the old duel-based "best units" ranking (`reports/aggregate.py`, attack at a floor, riposte at the mean): each round type of the play plan (`encounter.PLAN_ROUNDS`) at the **75 % floor** of its exact distribution (`encounter.FLOOR_COVERAGE`, user's choice, every unit; `smooth_floor` interpolates across the damage *step* — up to the next attainable damage value, 4 for a 4-damage weapon (2026-11-23, user's choice: interpolating over a single point hid most of a +1 to hit on few big hits — Avalenor's Firestealer Hammers +10 % instead of +43 %, mean +33 %; artillery with a few big shots rose most: Deathshrieker Rocket Battery 300th → 104th of the troops, top 10 almost unchanged) — so small effects stay measurable; dice-valued effects mix the two neighbouring integer profiles). Durability stays a mean; `unit_chassis` descriptors stay means. **Mobility axis** (`analysis/layers/mobility.py`, user: mobility = choosing the game's tempo, scoring, picking one's fights; GW barely prices Move — dropped from the cost model, p=0.44 — so a per-100-pts ranking without it rewards slow units, which put their points into wounds/save): `reach_in` = Move + 3.5" run + 2" FLY + the warscroll's own movement abilities (deep strike/redeploy/extra move +3", run-and-charge +1.5", free retreat +1", +N" Move; × activation probability, × ½ once per battle; working hypotheses); `mobility` = (reach / median reach)^`MOBILITY_WEIGHT` (0.35, user's choice: 0.25 roughly neutralised the slow-unit bias, 0.5 reversed it); `score_per_100` = `combined_per_100` × `mobility` is the default sort of `aospy encounter best` (`combined_per_100` kept, read by `listing.py`). Open question: one fight + one shoot per round (melee can fight in both turns but must reach contact first). Weight-1 unconditional effects are applied jointly to the profile (multiplicative), the rest are added as weighted gains on that boosted profile. Resources: Blood Tithe effects only cover the rounds from `ability.available_round`; any other faction-resource spend (`perimeter.SPENDS_RESOURCE` requirement, « Spend N … points », analyser v12) weighs 1/2 — one strong use at the kairos, an assumption to challenge. Tiers/options of one ability (« +1 … +2 instead ») keep only the strongest. Analyser v12 also tags wards limited to spells/prayers/manifestations or mortal damage (`target_condition` « vs sorts/manifestations » / « vs blessures mortelles ») — worth 0 against weapon attacks, so not translated (a layer-4 counter-combo) — reads requirements in a bullet list's header (« While this unit is within … of a friendly X: » → « près d'une unité amie »), and « has 1 damage point » as « porteur blessé ».

**Time axis** (`analysis/layers/timeline.py`, validated by the user — duration is not a 5th layer but the time axis crossing all layers): the game is a strip of 10 half-turns (R1 mine, R1 enemy … R5 enemy). Layer 1 projects *when an effect is active* (frequency, `ability.turn_side`, written duration: `span`); layer 3 *when the bearer triggers and holds it* (start round, survival); each half-turn has a *relevance* by polarity (offence: my turn for shooting + fighting, the enemy turn only for melee fight-back — half of a melee unit's fights; defence: `DEF_SHARE_ENEMY_TURN` = 0.7 of damage taken comes in the enemy turn); layer 4 will supply the round weights from the list's rhythm (`DEFAULT_RHYTHM` = 1/3, 1, 1, 1/3, 1/3). `realized.coverage`/`OwnEffect.weight(polarity, share_melee, start_round)` and `bearers.py` read it; the user's kairos rule is kept (once-per-battle short effect = 1/2 of a permanent one, spread over the share of the round its window covers: a one-turn ward played in the enemy turn ≈ 0.35, in mine ≈ 0.15), once-per-battle lasting effects start in round 2.

**Protections against incoming attacks** (2026-11-25, analyser v41): the durability (`unit_durability`) also reads what lowers the *enemy's* rolls against the unit, stored on `Unit.defence` (stat, enemy weapons « all »/« melee »/« ranged », value) by `encounter._incoming_transform`: −N to hit / wound / Rend / Damage / Attacks for attacks that target the unit (`side = enemy`, detail « defensif », and an effect **family** `buff-…` — `EffectSpec.family`, loaded from `ability_effect.family` — so a debuff put on one enemy unit, « the target's attacks », stays a debuff to read), `negate_attack` « unmodified 1-N fails » (hit or wound needs N+1 at least), `damage_ignore` first damage point per phase (+`FIRST_DAMAGE_PHASES` = 2 wounds, one round of shooting + combat) and Rend ignored (`save_neg`/`save_all`/`rend`). Measured against a reference attacker (`REF_HIT` 3+, `REF_WOUND` 3+, 3 attacks, Damage half 1 half 2, Rend 1, half melee half shooting): the hits-needed factor is the ratio of its damage per attack without and with the protections; one modifier per stat (±1 cap, reference Rend 1), the strongest kept. Without protections the durability is exactly `compute_effective_wounds`. Examples (4+ save): −1 to hit +33 %, −1 to hit vs shooting only +14 %, −1 Damage +50 %, fails on 1-4 +100 %. Not translated: a condition on the attacker (« vs MONSTER », `target_keywords`), Companion/named enemy weapons, untargetability, wards vs mortals. Layer 3 (`encounter build`, `own_effects`) now loads these enemy-side protections too (11 737 encounters, was ~9 100).
