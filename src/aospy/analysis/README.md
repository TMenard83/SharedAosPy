# analysis — modèle de coût et couches 2 à 4

Lire avant de modifier ce paquet (CLAUDE.md ne garde que la carte des couches).

## Modules

Two sub-packages, with an import-linter contract (`pyproject.toml`): `layers` may import `cost`, never the reverse.

- **`analysis/cost/`** — the cost model: `features.py`, `cost_model.py`, `scoring.py` (`combined_score`, the composite score shared by `reports/best_report.py` and `bonus_simulator/whatif.py`), `experiments/` (adopted A/B experiments).
- **`analysis/layers/`** — ability layers 2-4 (see "Architecture en quatre couches" below): `chassis`, `approach`, `timeline`, `encounter`, `realized`, `mobility`, `marriage`, `bearers`, `listing`, `assault`.

- **`analysis/cost/features.py`** / **`analysis/cost/cost_model.py`** (needs `[analysis]`: pandas + statsmodels). `features.py::compute_features` probes a unit against three synthetic defenders (save 2+/4+/none) via `combat.py`'s moments engine → `UnitFeatures`. `cost_model.py` regresses `points ~ features` (OLS) three ways (`fit_cost_model`, `fit_segmented`, `fit_cost_model_factorial`) for coefficients + per-unit residuals. See "Cost model" below.

## Cost model (`analysis/cost/features.py` / `analysis/cost/cost_model.py`) — scope vs StatHammer

Ported from StatHammer's own modules, adapted to aospy's already-typed schema (no text parsing needed for `move`/`save`/`control`). `MODEL_FEATURES`/`CATEGORICAL_FEATURES` in `cost_model.py` document, inline, the rationale behind each predictor choice — read those comments before adding/removing a feature. The A/B experiments backing each choice are runnable code: `analysis/cost/experiments/*.py` holds the ones that were adopted (each derives its "before" baseline from the current, already-adopted `MODEL_FEATURES`/`CATEGORICAL_FEATURES` rather than hardcoding a snapshot, so the comparison stays valid as those constants evolve); the rejected ones were deleted (git history only). Three fitting strategies share `feature_frame`: `fit_cost_model` (single OLS), `fit_segmented` (separate hero/troupe regressions), `fit_cost_model_factorial` (one OLS with categorical factors + `C(is_hero):...` interaction terms — only the first two are wired to `aospy cost fit`).

**Control is priced as its deviation from GW's tacit design rule** (`features.expected_control`, reconstructed with the user): a profile's *expected* control (light infantry 1, ≥5-HP infantry 2, hero 2, BEAST 1, MONSTER 5, ≥12-HP war machine 5, Ogor/Gargant monsters 10, banner heroes 5…) is **not** paid (p≈0.1); only `control_bonus` (epic models at 10-15, a few named "leader" characters, ≈+14 pts/point, p<0.001) and `control_deficit` (10-HP mounted/chariot heroes kept at 2, ≈−8 pts/point) enter `MODEL_FEATURES`. Raw `control` conflated control with unit type/model size (R² adj 0.8575 → 0.8600; banner heroes no longer look under-costed). Experiment: `analysis/cost/experiments/control_profile.py`. The rule partly keys on names (banners, Loonboss on Giant Cave Squig) and armies (Ogor Mawtribes, Sons of Behemat): extend it when a new battletome adds such profiles. For *ability* scoring control stays a weak stat regardless of its price (user's table-value rule).

Deliberately **not** ported: an ability-power axis (StatHammer's LLM-scored `ability_scores.py`) and a tactical-role ranking (`roles.py`) — aospy stores no per-unit ability text, only a free-text Crit tag per weapon. `wizard_level`/`priest_level` (from the `WIZARD (N)`/`PRIEST (N)` keywords) are the one exception, giving casters a direct — if narrow — ability-power proxy.

`CostModelResult.model` (the fitted `statsmodels` object, populated by both `fit_cost_model`/`fit_cost_model_factorial`) and `predict_one(result, features)` exist purely so `bonus_simulator/whatif.py` can predict a cost for an in-memory, not-yet-persisted `UnitFeatures` row (e.g. a unit with a bonus applied) by reusing patsy's formula-encoding rather than hand-rolling the categorical dummies. Neither is used by `aospy cost fit`/`cost residuals` or the PDF reports, which only read `coefficients`/`residuals`.

## Architecture en quatre couches

La couche 1 (aptitude hors contexte) est décrite dans `../abilities/README.md`.

**Four-layer architecture (user's design) — keep the layers separate:**

1. **Ability, ex nihilo** (this package, done): judged without knowing who carries or receives it. A warscroll *stat* is given; an ability *bonus* has to be played/triggered — never value an ability by applying it to a "median unit" (rejected proposal: that sneaks context into layer 1). Power stays a qualitative, user-calibrated grid; quantified effects only record their raw magnitude plus their **perimeter** (below). Manifestations are the known hard case (summon spell → manifestation → its own abilities), approximated as a chain.
2. **Chassis** (`analysis/layers/chassis.py`, table `unit_chassis`, `aospy chassis build|list|show`): the bare warscroll — HP, save, weapon profiles. Two complementary tools: the combat engine measures what the chassis *does* (`dmg_vs_save4`, `effective_wounds`), the cost model measures what GW *charges* for it. Measured on every unit with `bonus_simulator.apply_bonus`: GW prices defence at ≈½ of its mechanical gain (ward 5+ = +50 % durability, +25-30 % points) but offence at ≈⅛ (+1 hit = +28 % damage, +4 % points; damage coefficients are weak, p≈0.12) — so offensive chassis may be structurally cheap, to be confirmed. `ChassisProfile` stores performance (melee/ranged damage per round vs save 4+, effective wounds, both per 100 pts with a centile rank within heroes/troops) plus the **compatibility descriptors** layer 3 reads to know whether an ability "takes" on a warscroll: `share_damage1` (share of damage from 1-damage weapons — what "+1 Damage" doubles), `share_companion` (Companion weapons, insensitive to most buffs), `share_melee`, `attacks_total`, dominant `crit_type`. The cost-model residual and duel efficiency (`unit_benchmark`, same reading as `reports/aggregate.py`) are joined when available. No composite chassis score yet (the offence/defence weighting is still open).
3. **Ability × chassis encounter** (`analysis/layers/encounter.py`, table `ability_encounter`, `aospy encounter build|list`): what a *friendly* effect really yields on *this* warscroll, via the engine — +1 Damage on 1-damage weapons is +100 %, on 3-damage +33 %; +N Attacks on a Companion weapon is nothing. Each effect becomes a **profile rewrite** (copy of the `Unit`, like `bonus_simulator.apply_bonus`, extended to the effect perimeter: melee/ranged/named weapon, `chosen` = best weapon, Companion excluded unless stated; hit/wound/save modifiers capped at ±1; crits/Anti-X/Charge appended to `Weapon.abilities` text, which `combat.py` already parses; wards don't stack) — `combat.py` untouched. Measured before/after with the chassis probes (round damage vs save 4+, effective wounds), stored as **conditional** gains (« if it applies »): reliability/duration/prerequisites stay as columns to weight later. Receivers: own warscroll for `self`, army units satisfying `ability.friendly_target` (analyser v6: the friendly-target restriction as written, « non-HERO WARRIORS OF CHAOS INFANTRY », resolved against unit keywords — a keyword no warscroll carries, e.g. one gained in play, doesn't filter) for friendly scopes, heroes for traits/artefacts/enhancements; `source` keeps the owner group (formation, lore, Army of Renown…) since those only apply when chosen. `fight_twice` (analyser v8: « can use 2 FIGHT abilities this phase/turn », e.g. Gunnar Brand every turn, Battle-fury once per battle) = melee output ×2. Not translated (v1): `extra_fight` (« can be picked to use a FIGHT ability immediately after », activation chaining: tempo, not output), enemy-side debuffs, rerolls (engine has none), « ignore the first damage point », manifestations. **Realized value / best units** (`analysis/layers/realized.py`, table `unit_realized`, `aospy encounter best`): chassis + the warscroll's *own* abilities that boost the unit itself (`self`, or a friendly bubble/order it is an eligible target of — `ability.excludes_self`, analyser v10, drops « another/other friendly »); heroic traits/artefacts/army rules/other units' buffs are list-level (layer 4). Weights validated by the user: rounds 2-3 weigh 1, rounds 1/4/5 weigh 1/3 (opening, mop-up, scoring); passive/permanent or reusable every turn/phase/round → 1 (« once per turn (army) » only prevents spamming across several units: list-level, not here); once per battle short effect → 1/2 (played at the kairos, the tipping point); once per battle lasting the rest of the battle → 8/9 (activated round 2); × `activation_prob` (prayers: `extract.p_prayer_banked`, ritual points banked in round 1 then chanted at the key round — Core Rules p. 34 — and prayers can't be unbound). Conditions (charged, vs HERO, on objective…) get no weight: they are interactions that orient the list, so they're excluded from `*_realized` and added in `*_good_case`. Offence is read through a **play plan** (`analysis/layers/approach.py`, `encounter.play_plan`, user request « modélise l'approche, la portée est un facteur important »): the 5 rounds are simulated from the season's 18" start gap — melee plan: move (fly included), shoot what is in range, charge (2D6 ≥ remaining gap) or run if nothing is possible, then stay in contact, shooting only « Shoot in Combat » guns; shooting plan per distinct weapon range L: stand at L" (round 1: no closer than start gap − move), shoot what reaches, charged each enemy turn with `ENEMY_CHARGE_SHARE` (0.5) × P(2D6 ≥ L − `ENEMY_MOVE` 6"), then stuck in combat. The enemy advances 6" per turn. A `Plan` = round types (share of the game weighted by `DEFAULT_RHYTHM`, set of weapons striking), each read at the `FLOOR_COVERAGE` floor; the bare chassis picks its best plan, effects are measured on that same plan. Effect on the ranking: pure melee −11 % offence (round 1 spent approaching), 18-24" shooters up, 10-12" shooters down. Hypotheses to challenge: those constants, I play first in round 1, a charged shooter never gets free. Per-family damage goes through `features.weapon_damages` (weapon counts resolved on the *whole* unit: measuring a weapon subset alone re-resolves « 1 of the following » choices per subset — Stormfiends read 21.5 instead of 14.2); the cost model's `dmg_melee_*`/`dmg_ranged_*` features and `dominant_crit_type` use it too (R² adj 0.879 → 0.880 vs per-subset resolution, Stormfiends' predicted cost 333 → 311). Ranking indicator (user-validated): `combined_per_100` = √(damage/100 × durability/100) (Lanchester logic: what a unit deals over its lifetime needs both — glass cannons such as Loonsmasha Fanatics no longer top it), ranked separately for troops and heroes (`combined_pct_rank`; heroes pay for support abilities outside this scope). Damage is read like the old duel-based "best units" ranking (`reports/aggregate.py`, attack at a floor, riposte at the mean): each round type of the play plan (`encounter.PLAN_ROUNDS`) at the **75 % floor** of its exact distribution (`encounter.FLOOR_COVERAGE`, user's choice, every unit; `smooth_floor` interpolates between integers so small effects stay measurable; dice-valued effects mix the two neighbouring integer profiles). Durability stays a mean; `unit_chassis` descriptors stay means. **Mobility axis** (`analysis/layers/mobility.py`, user: mobility = choosing the game's tempo, scoring, picking one's fights; GW barely prices Move — dropped from the cost model, p=0.44 — so a per-100-pts ranking without it rewards slow units, which put their points into wounds/save): `reach_in` = Move + 3.5" run + 2" FLY + the warscroll's own movement abilities (deep strike/redeploy/extra move +3", run-and-charge +1.5", free retreat +1", +N" Move; × activation probability, × ½ once per battle; working hypotheses); `mobility` = (reach / median reach)^`MOBILITY_WEIGHT` (0.35, user's choice: 0.25 roughly neutralised the slow-unit bias, 0.5 reversed it); `score_per_100` = `combined_per_100` × `mobility` is the default sort of `aospy encounter best` (`combined_per_100` kept, read by `listing.py`). Open question: one fight + one shoot per round (melee can fight in both turns but must reach contact first). Weight-1 unconditional effects are applied jointly to the profile (multiplicative), the rest are added as weighted gains on that boosted profile. Resources: Blood Tithe effects only cover the rounds from `ability.available_round`; any other faction-resource spend (`perimeter.SPENDS_RESOURCE` requirement, « Spend N … points », analyser v12) weighs 1/2 — one strong use at the kairos, an assumption to challenge. Tiers/options of one ability (« +1 … +2 instead ») keep only the strongest. Analyser v12 also tags wards limited to spells/prayers/manifestations or mortal damage (`target_condition` « vs sorts/manifestations » / « vs blessures mortelles ») — worth 0 against weapon attacks, so not translated (a layer-4 counter-combo) — reads requirements in a bullet list's header (« While this unit is within … of a friendly X: » → « près d'une unité amie »), and « has 1 damage point » as « porteur blessé ».
   **Time axis** (`analysis/layers/timeline.py`, validated by the user — duration is not a 5th layer but the time axis crossing all layers): the game is a strip of 10 half-turns (R1 mine, R1 enemy … R5 enemy). Layer 1 projects *when an effect is active* (frequency, `ability.turn_side`, written duration: `span`); layer 3 *when the bearer triggers and holds it* (start round, survival); each half-turn has a *relevance* by polarity (offence: my turn for shooting + fighting, the enemy turn only for melee fight-back — half of a melee unit's fights; defence: `DEF_SHARE_ENEMY_TURN` = 0.7 of damage taken comes in the enemy turn); layer 4 will supply the round weights from the list's rhythm (`DEFAULT_RHYTHM` = 1/3, 1, 1, 1/3, 1/3). `realized.coverage`/`OwnEffect.weight(polarity, share_melee, start_round)` and `bearers.py` read it; the user's kairos rule is kept (once-per-battle short effect = 1/2 of a permanent one, spread over the share of the round its window covers: a one-turn ward played in the enemy turn ≈ 0.35, in mine ≈ 0.15), once-per-battle lasting effects start in round 2.
   **Chassis × ability marriage** (`analysis/layers/marriage.py`, table `ability_marriage`, `aospy encounter marriage`, user's reframing): a good ability can be badly married (Oath of Supremacy on a 5"/5-HP Darkoath Warqueen). Each ability has a functional **family** (`abilities/families.py`, `ability.family`/`ability_effect.family`: buff-perso-offensif/defensif, buff-donne, debuff, degats-directs, mobilite, soin, controle, magie-ressource, autre). Marriage = **D × S**: D = P(the *bearer* meets its own conditions — charged via 2D6 ≥ gap − move, gap = 18" in round 1 then 14" (`ENGAGE_GAP_ROUND1`/`ENGAGE_GAP`), enemy territory reached after 9" + footprint (`TERRITORY_GAP`) — distances per season (`SEASON_GEOMETRY`, they change with each General's Handbook) — 2026-27 checked on its 12 battleplans: territories always touch, units deploy more than 9" from enemy territory (Deploy Unit, Core Rules p. 13), on objective, in combat, not moved/not charged) × share of rounds still possible; S = share of rounds the bearer survives to hold the effect, at the exposure its range imposes (contact ≤3", short ≤9", long ≥10" behind a screen) under staged pressure — 1 average enemy unit in round 1, 2 in rounds 2-3, 1 in rounds 4-5, damage accumulating (`ATTACKERS_PER_ROUND`, user's choice) — from `unit_benchmark` riposte split melee/ranged (estimated from effective wounds when absent); a « charged » bonus counts for half of a unit's fights (`CHARGE_FIGHT_SHARE`, user's choice). Conditions held by others (ally → layer 4, opponent → matchups, resource) are not priced, only listed. `unit_realized.*_fit*` (« ch. » columns of `encounter best`) = realized + bearer-held conditional effects × D. Every constant in `marriage.py` is a working hypothesis to challenge. Layer 3 judges whether a *bearer* carries a support ability well; whether anyone benefits is layer 4 (user's banner example). **Good bearers of enhancements** (`analysis/layers/bearers.py`, table `enhancement_bearer`, `aospy encounter bearers --ability X | --unit X | --army X`): every heroic trait/artefact/enhancement/monstrous trait × every eligible unit of its faction, ranked (user: « les bons porteurs », a list, not one winner). Eligibility = `ability.bearer_restriction`, read from the Wahapedia group/type description (`source.bearer_restriction_of`: « HERO only », « Non-OGOR IRONSUNZ HERO only », « … can be given to non-Hero X Infantry or Cavalry units », named warscrolls, « with the STANDARD BEARER keyword », « excluding … »; a stray « non- HERO » space is normalised), default HERO (HERO MONSTER for monstrous traits); UNIQUE units never (Core Rules p. 37). Value = absolute gain of √(damage × durability) on that bearer (enhancements cost no points) × coverage × marriage; enhancements with no translatable effect (bubbles, spells…) are ranked by marriage then by the bearer's combined indicator. Several abilities share a name across groups (artefact vs Army-of-Renown enhancement): listed separately.
4. **Abilities × army** (`analysis/layers/listing.py`, `aospy roster analyze --list <file> [--out x.md]`, Markdown via `cli/report.format_list_report`): the *numbers* a human list analysis builds on — never the analysis itself (concept, roles, round-by-round plan stay hand-written, user's method in memory `list-building-concept`). Per unit: layer-3 values and centile ranks, a suggested role (hammer / glass cannon / anvil / caster / support / specialist when the good case ≥ 1.3 × realized), earliest engagement round, abilities badly married to their bearer (`ability_marriage` < 0.4); enhancements taken vs the best eligible bearer *in the list* (`enhancement_bearer`); dead rules (prayer/spell lore with no priest/wizard, friendly-target abilities with no receiver in the list); list effects × list receivers weighted on the time axis × trigger D (a « charged » condition limits the effect to my turn — the time axis then halves it for a melee unit, no separate charge share); army-wide limits; magic budget; abilities of the manifestations the chosen lore can summon (`ListAbility.summon_prob` = P of the SUMMON spell: a source posed by a manifestation only exists if the summon passes). **Assault** (`analysis/layers/assault.py`, calibrated on the user's own Hibou_2 analysis: a grouped-charge plan holds by *redundancy* and *fallbacks*, not by one charge's reliability): chargers = the list's melee hitters (`share_melee` ≥ 0.5, melee damage ≥ ½ of the best), charge bonuses (`ability_effect.stat = 'charge'`) and full charge rerolls (`reroll` with `stat = 'charge'`, analyser v25; a partial one-die reroll isn't counted) — receivers from the effect's own `ability_effect.friendly_target` (v26), else the target the sentence writes, else the ability's, dice-count effects (`dice_count`, analysers v28-v29: 3D6 summed, 3 dice keep the best 2, lowest D6 set to the highest — exact probabilities, a 9" charge is 74 % / 52 % / 56 % vs 28 % on 2D6; the best available mode is taken per distance, their order changes with it), the generic reroll command (1 CP, one charge per turn, rerolls the most promising failure); exact P(≥ k successes of N) after a teleport (> 9" → a 9 suffices); on foot, a charge is not a plan but a punishment of the opponent's mistakes (user), so it is shown as a **threat range** per charger (`Threat`: move + longest charge made at ≥ 50 %, dry / with bonuses / with reroll); with the summon probability and the teleport spell's success folded in. Not counted: enemy unbinding, second teleport attempts, casting order (stays hand-written). Worked example: `scratch/output/listes/Hibou_2 - fiche chiffrée.md`; the hand analysis it was calibrated against is in `data/2026-27 L3 Aller/Listes/Listes Melusior.md` (« Analyse stratégique »).
