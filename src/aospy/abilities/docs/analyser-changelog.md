# Journal de l'analyseur (v33 →)

Déplacé de `abilities/README.md`. Une nouvelle version de l'analyseur ajoute sa section `**Analyser vN**` ici, en tête (plus récente d'abord) ; ne lire ce fichier que pour retrouver pourquoi une règle de lecture existe (ou `git log -S`).

**Analyser v33** (defects found by layer 3's hero-support value):
- **Mortal damage**: `ability_effect.value` is now the expected value when the count is known. « Roll 10 dice. For each 4+, inflict 1 » = 5. « Roll a D3. On a 2+, … equal to the roll » = 5/3. A D6 gate already counted in `activation_prob` gives the mean given that the gate passed (D6 on 2+ = 4). When the number of dice depends on the target or the context, `value` = P(N+) × K per die, with `detail` = `par dé`, weighted like the former « BM variables ». Before v33 that case was stored as a value-0 row (with `dice` = « par jet », a positional-argument slip) plus a duplicate value-1 row. The per-roll regex also reads « allocate N mortal damage ». Mortal damage « on the target » when the declared target is friendly (Foul Elixirs: « On a 1, inflict D3 ») is a self-inflicted drawback.
- **Exclusive options** (`ability_effect.option_group`, `targets.option_of`): an option or bullet line *after* « pick 1 of the following », « the effect below that corresponds… » or « choose one ». Scoring takes the best option (`scoring.power_of`). Layer 3 keeps one option per ability: `realized.strongest_alternatives` keeps the option with the most translatable effects, and `support_values` keeps the best option per receiver.
- **Bearer-only effect**: « … and this unit has WARD (4+) » in an ability aimed at another friendly unit (Royal Retinue). `Effect.friendly_target` is set to the bearer's warscroll name, which layer 3 resolves like any warscroll name. For a faction rule there is no warscroll name, so the ability's own target applies.
- **Conditions**: a SENTENCED target becomes the `target_condition` « cible désignée » (Judge, Jury and Executioner). A target « passed across this phase » (Bomb Racks) becomes the set-up condition « doit survoler la cible » and the requirement `a survolé la cible`. « does not have FLY » no longer reads as FLY. « … while they have STRIKE-FIRST » (Rune of Fury) is the requirement `frappe en premier`, not a Strike-first gain. Master of the Runes: Wahapedia truncates the text at « Rune of Searing Heat: Add 1 to t ». The full text (5 runes) is in `source_corrections.json`, taken from the French Faction Pack p. 13.
- **Chained fights** (user's choice 2026-11-22, option « ×0.5 »): « The target can be picked to fight immediately after… If it is picked to do so, add 1 to hit » (Brawlerguts, Lead Da Brutes, Infantry Overseer…) gets the requirement `combat enchaîné`. Layer 3 does not drop it like other conditions: it weighs it by `analysis.layers.marriage.CHAINED_FIGHT_SHARE` (0.5, the share of fights where the hero and the unit fight one after the other — a hypothesis to calibrate), in `marriage.trigger` and in `realized.support_values`.

**Analyser v34** (end of layer 1, items A and D):
- **Truncated texts**: 20 of the 35 « a-revoir » abilities whose Wahapedia text has no final punctuation are completed in `data/json/source_corrections.json` (scan, French verbatim of `faction_rules_fr.json`, or the English October 2026 rules update; page in `source`). 6 were not cut (a list or a missing final period). 9 have no scan here: OATH OF RESTORATION (Duardin Ascendant, White Dwarf 506), WYRDFLAME BARRAGE (Pyrofane Cult, White Dwarf 514, Legends since October 2026), DELUSION AND DISCIPLINE and INSANITY AND NIGHTMARES MADE MANIFEST (Legions of Nagash), BACKSTABBING ALLIES (Gathering of the Clans), WARDENS OF THE WOLF (Legions of Ulfenkarn), ENERGY SURGE, Rip It Up and Chuck It, Rock Bottom (Sons of Behemat battletome).
- **Cut-off texts, every verdict (« A bis »)**: Wahapedia's export cuts `description` at 1024 raw characters (HTML included). So the test is the raw length (≥ 990), not the final punctuation: a cut can fall right after a full stop (Seasons of War lost 2 of its 4 seasons). 245 abilities reach the limit; 121 more fixes went into `source_corrections.json`:
  - 90 from the French verbatim of `faction_rules_fr.json`;
  - 15 from the English October 2026 rules update;
  - 11 from text-layer PDFs (armies of renown, Scourge of Aqshy, regiments of renown);
  - 5 standard formulas cut in their last words.

  50 were complete (the length belongs to a same-name variant). 68 have no source: the Sons of Behemat, Ossiarch Bonereapers and Helsmiths battletomes are not scanned, and neither are the Legions of Nagash, Champions of the Arena, Zainthar Kai and Bloodshadow Rites lists. Identical full texts now merge (3 630 → 3 627 abilities). Two notes-only overrides were re-keyed (Seething Blight, You First, I Insist...).
- **Negated heal/return**: « If an ability would heal or return any slain models to X, that ability does not heal (any damage points) or return any slain models to it » is an `anti_heal`, not a friendly `return_models`. Before v34, Desperate Remedies, Atavistic Nightmares, Fractured Astral Jewel and Deluge of Nurgle were overvalued because of this.
- **Enemy charge**: ignoring the Charge (+1 Damage) weapon ability of enemy attacks (Anticipated Strike, Protective Pike-Wall) only matters when the enemy charged → `perimeter._ENEMY_CHARGE_WEAPON`, requirement `charge ennemie`.
- **Either charge**: « if it charged … or if it is in combat with an enemy unit that charged » (Brutal Troof) is one condition, `perimeter.CHARGE_EITHER` (« a chargé ou charge ennemie »), ease penalty 0.5 (the easier of the two). Layer 3 reads it as a charge-turn fight with p = 1 (`marriage.BEARER_CONDITIONS`).
- **One-time trigger**: « Once Per Battle » + an effect starting « If …, » that lasts « for the rest of the battle » (Oath of Supremacy, Darkoath oaths, Rune of Restored Hearth): the « If » conditions are met once. Their ease penalties are halved (`extract.ONCE_TRIGGER_PENALTY_FACTOR`, my choice, to calibrate), and the effect's requirement becomes `déclencheur unique (charge, en territoire)`. It deliberately does not contain « a chargé », so layer 3 does not simulate a charge-turn fight. `marriage.trigger` gives p = 1 from a start round (`territory_round`, else `ONCE_TRIGGER_START` = 2). Oath of Supremacy goes from bonus to plan: its power (1.8, the same as passive +1 hit 12" bubbles since v13) was never the issue.
- **Random result tables** (« Roll a dice and apply the corresponding effect: 1 - …, 2-4 - … », `targets.dice_row_of`): each row is an option `dé 2-4`. A row « In addition to the effect of … » carries the previous row's effects (`dé 5-6 +préc`). Power = expected value over the rows (D6, or 2D6 when a range goes past 6), or the best of k dice for « Roll 2 dice, pick either result » (`scoring._table_power`). Before v34 every row counted at once (Aura of Mutation 2.9, Gorbolga 2.9). A table needs a roll: the Cogforts' « 1 Launch Countermeasures! » rows are heat-token costs. Layer 3 still keeps the strongest row (`realized.strongest_alternatives`).
- **Faction mechanics (item B, user's rules of 2026-11-22)**: most « a-revoir » abilities draw their value from *other* abilities. `extract/references.py` detects them, but only when nothing else was recognized. `abilities/references.py` resolves them in a second pass over the whole base (`RESOLUTION_PASSES` = 2, so a chain like HEAD GARDENER → 'Cultivating the Garden of Nurgle' → Gnarlmaw also resolves). The effect's `value` is the resolved power. A reference that cannot be found (core command 'Power Through', season rules such as 'Eruption of Fury') is dropped and the ability stays « a-revoir »: it is never guessed.
  - `ability_ref`: modifies a named ability. The target is read from a quoted name (possessive apostrophes and names like 'Eat 'Em Alive' are handled), a keyword group (CYCLE, ASTERISM…), a faction roll (« shield bash rolls ») or an alias (faces of the Bad Moon, delusion roll). Power is a graduated fraction of the cited ability's power (mean if several): +1 to its roll or more range = 1/3, an extra use, target or eligibility = 1/2, the effect applied in full (« as if active ») = 1. A full reference reads the cited power without its rotation factor.
  - `status_grant`: under orders, unified, anointed, Pledge to Chaos, POWER OF THE WAAAGH!, quarry, mark, runes, overgrowth… Power = mean power of the faction abilities that read the status (`STATUSES`), then the usual scope and frequency. An enemy-side scope (« for each enemy unit destroyed ») does not multiply.
  - `free_enhancement`: « give the target 1 artefact of power from the 'X' ». Power = mean of table X (fallback: the faction's abilities of that kind) × number of heroes, capped at 3, with no once-per-battle penalty.
  - `terrain_setup`: an extra faction terrain piece = 1/2 × mean power of its warscroll's abilities.
  - `warscroll_rule` (0): a token placed by an active ability (Grype, Bomb Rats… the value is in the sibling ability that reads it), and rule frameworks (delusion roll, Bad Moon faces, Cycle sequence, Great Plan, Facets of War, blood rites, dirty-trick rule, KO transport capacity, Fall from Grace, a Regiment of Renown's own terrain deployment).
  - Rotation engines (`Extraction.power_factor`): a DELUSION is only believed on some turns (×5/6); a CYCLE ability is only ripe in some rounds (×1/3). A DIRTY TRICK needs a success roll (activation × 2/3, the table is cut and no scan exists).
  - Exclusive options: « pick 1 of the effects below » (HoS Temptations) and challenges (« accept or decline », You First, I Insist...) are now options. When the opponent picks (`targets.OPPONENT_PICKS`), power is the weakest option. A random-table row « Pick any other effect » (Aura of Mutation) is worth the best other row.
  - Generic reading fixes found on the way: in « add 1 to X and add 1 to Y », the second modifier is now read (13 abilities: MONSTER HUNTERS, THEIR FINEST HOUR, DRAIN VITALITY…); « Inict » → « Inflict ».
  - 14 isolated cases are overrides (DOUBLE STOMP, BRUTE SPIRITUALITY, ARCANE FOCUS, Master of the Bone-Tithe…). PLAGUE PROGENITOR was re-read: the anti-heal does hit the enemy progenitor, so it only gets a note.
  - Result: a-revoir 194 → 43.
- **Faction resources (item C, user's rates of 2026-11-22)**: `abilities/resources.py`. `RESOURCE_RATES` (re-exported by `pipeline`) covers unlocks (Blood Tithe, 2/round → available round). `SPEND_RATES` covers resources spent on each use, in points per round: rage dice 2 (= the mean fury level, `FURY_LEVEL`), relentless discipline 3, fate 1.5 (also the « Fate » cost type), raw ingredients 8, oath 1, asydrazor 2, noble deeds 3, disease 3, blood tithe points 2. An ability that spends N gets a usage factor min(1, rate / N) on its power, stored in `ability.resource_usage` for layer 3 (in place of the flat 1/2 when the rate is known). Competition for the same pool is left to layer 4, like the CP budget. The fury level counts as 2 in modifiers (« half your fury level » = 1). Lowering the opponent's fury level is `fury_debuff`: −1 = 0.5, reset to 0 = 1.0. a-revoir 43 → 39. Still unread: per-unit tokens with no generic rate (heat, leech, stored time, sacrifice, DPP tables cut by Wahapedia), and the cut « cumulative effects depending on your fury level » tables.

**Analyser v42** (contrôles des analyses de listes L3, 2026-11-25) : « Pick a friendly IRONJAWZ HERO to be the target … the following effects apply to friendly IRONJAWZ units while they are wholly within 18" of the target » — les effets sans cible propre prennent les receveurs de l'en-tête (`extract._HEADER_RECEIVERS` : Ironjawz Waaagh!, Grunta Waaagh!, qui ne buffaient que les héros) ; « For each friendly X unit … that was destroyed in the previous turn, you can pick … » ajoute le prérequis `perimeter.AFTER_FRIENDLY_LOSS` (« après une perte amie », Memories of the Fallen, Envoy of the Heavens), pondéré par la couche 3 (`marriage.P_AFTER_FRIENDLY_LOSS` = 0,5 à partir du round 2, hypothèse à calibrer). Le reste des corrections du jour est en couche 4 (`analysis/docs/listing.md`).

**Analyser v41** (protections that the engine lacked, 2026-11-25): `negate_attack` carries its threshold — « if the unmodified hit roll [or unmodified wound roll] for an attack that targets this unit is 1-N, the attack fails » → `stat` « hit » / « wound » / « hit,wound », `value` N (`effects.protection_reading`; « that ability has no effect » and Warp Lightning Blast stay without one) ; `damage_ignore` gets a sub-kind in `stat`: `first_damage` (« ignore the first damage point … each phase »), `save_neg` / `save_all` (« ignore [negative] modifiers to save rolls »), `rend` (« ignore the Rend characteristic »). Read by the layer-3 engine (`encounter._incoming_transform`).

**Analyser v40** (found by the review of 12 lists): « Companion weapons used by … », « Companion melee weapons », « Attacks characteristic of Companion weapons » target ONLY the Companion weapons (`weapon_scope` « companion », `includes_companion`; « the Companion weapon ability » is not a weapon scope) — they were read as « all weapons, Companion excluded », the opposite.

**Analyser v39** (found by crossing a list's rules, layer 4): « If the target is BLOODBOUND / a non-MONSTER DAEMON unit / a MONSTER, … » on an option line is that effect's friendly target (`targets.effect_friendly_target`, Blood-drenched); « Ignore positive modifiers to save rolls for the target » on a friendly declared target is a drawback (`no_buff`, detail `contrepartie`, family `autre`, worth 0); « Pick another visible friendly … » sets `excludes_self` (13 abilities, Commander of Tyrants).

**Analyser v38** (found by the layer-4 chains): « friendly SKAVEN INFANTRY and CAVALRY units » reads army-wide (`scope._ARMY_KW` accepts keywords coordinated by and/or), but not after « of any » (« within 12" of any friendly SKINK INFANTRY or SKINK CAVALRY units » is a condition: Spawn of Sotek, Scryhawks); « While/If/Each time a friendly X is/are/uses … » with no « this unit » in the first sentence and no enemy units in the text is army-wide (INDULGE, MY PRETTIES, Hide-shelter!, HERD HEALING, GUN BUTT LOW BLOW…), not `self`.

**Analyser v37** (reading fixes found by the layer-3 magic grid):
- **Set-up from reserve is a deep strike, not a summon**: « Set up this unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 9" » (Emergence, Chameleon Ambush, Grinderfist Tunnellers, Loathsome Descent…), « Set up this unit within 6" of an enemy unit » (Sudden Ambush), « Set up this unit more than 6" from the target » and « set up the (other) units that were … with this unit » (Trespassers!) are `deep_strike` (family `mobilite`); `summon` no longer matches them, so a set-up is no longer counted twice (deep strike + summon: 2.52 → 2.0); a replacement unit stays a summon.
- **Power-level malus** (« While this unit has 10 or more damage points, subtract 1 from its power level », Battle Damaged) is a drawback, family `autre`.

**Analyser v36** (reading fixes found by the layer-3 mobility grid):
- **Target picked friendly OR enemy, re-designated enemy** in its paragraph (« If the target is an enemy unit, inflict D3 mortal damage on it », Invocation of Nagash, Gifts of Pain and Pleasure, Retribution or Salvation; « If you picked an enemy unit to be the target, … », Jolly Gutpipes): mortal damage on the enemy, no longer a self-inflicted cost (`effects._ENEMY_REDESIGNATED`).
- **Not a set-up**: « cannot (move or) be removed from the battlefield by an ability that would allow it to be set up again » is a restriction (enemy: Fetters of Blood, Snuggling Sickness, The Mirror's Depths; own drawback: Awakening Slumbering Spirits), and « Each time the target is removed from the battlefield and set up again » a trigger (`effects._NOT_A_SETUP`); « that RETREAT ability has no effect » is an enemy restriction.
- **The opponent moves its own unit** (« Your opponent must remove the target … set it up again in combat with this unit », Shadow Lariat, DR AG 'EM OUT): `redeploy` on the enemy side → family `debuff`.
- **Subject resolution** (`_subject_side`): « that unit » is a pronoun; an antecedent « Pick a visible enemy unit » earlier on the same option line counts (Mesmerise, Gobbapalooza Know-Wotz); « While/If the target …, it … » follows the target, not « this unit » written later in the sentence; « … and it cannot … » opens a new clause.
- **« au combat »**: « If this unit is in combat, pick … » heading the Declare applies to all effects (Terminate Their Command, Storm of Steel, Grind Them to Dust, Lead From The Front); « If this unit is in combat or charged this turn » is « au combat » alone, not « a chargé » AND « au combat » (Windrider, Smoke Bombs).
- Override (isolated phrasing): Awakening Slumbering Spirits (+D3 to casting rolls, unread) → correct 1.5.
- **Healing readings** (found by the layer-3 healing grid): dice before integers in `_HEAL_RE`/`_RETURN_RE` (« Heal (2D6) » read 2: Lifebloom, Horribly Resilient, The Hunger), « return up to D3 models », 3D3/4D3/4D6/D6+2 added to `dice._DICE_MEAN`; « an ability that would heal 1 or more » is a trigger, « add 1 to the number of damage points healed » a friendly heal bonus (detail `bonus aux soins`), « subtract 1 from the number of damage points healed » an enemy `anti_heal` (Polluting Aura); a heal « by this unit's combat attacks » / « each time this unit uses a FIGHT ability » requires `au combat`, and one after destroying an enemy HERO/WIZARD/PRIEST/MONSTER gets `target_condition` « élimine un … » (`extract._heal_kill_condition`).
- **Singular friendly bubble** (`scope._FRIENDLY_SINGLE_AURA`): « If/Each time a friendly X unit wholly within 12" of this unit … » is `friendly_aura` 12", not `self` (17 Rally bonuses, a few magic relays).
- **Control readings** (found by the layer-3 control grid): a `control` « control score » effect is enemy-side after « enemy » in its sentence or on an enemy target (« that enemy unit has a maximum control score of 1 », Omens of Decay, Scorched Remains) → family `debuff`, unless it is « this unit's/its control score » (Light A Pyre); « This unit has a maximum control score of 1/'-' » is a drawback (detail `contrepartie`, worth 0, family `autre`: Beast on 20 warscrolls, Expendable Munitions) and no longer hides the next option (Transplanted Brains); « Ignore positive modifiers to the target's control score and to hit rolls, wound rolls and save rolls » is `no_buff` (Grief-stricken); the bearer's shooting is also read in a reaction trigger (« Reaction: You declared a SHOOT ability for this unit »: Barbed Arrows, Deathly Barrage, Specialist Grundstok Ammunition held at shooting range, no longer at contact).
- **Friendly models sacrificed** (« Up to 3 models in the drained unit are automatically slain », both targets friendly: Father of Necromancy, Executioner and Enforcer): a friendly `slay` with detail `auto-infligé`, family `autre`, worth 0 — Father of Necromancy is now a heal.

**Analyser v35** (reading fixes found by the layer-3 debuff grid):
- **Declare conditions on enemy targets**: the charge written in the Declare (« If this unit charged this turn, pick an enemy unit in combat with it », « Pick an enemy unit that charged this turn », « If this unit did not charge this turn ») now reaches the *enemy-side* effects too (`extract.declare_requires`: `a chargé` / `charge ennemie` / `sans charge`); v23 only handled friendly effects. ~18 debuffs and the charge-impact mortal damage (Crashing Impact, Slaughterous Charge…) gain the requirement; layer 3 then reads D through P(charge).
- **« and that charged »**: in « an enemy unit in combat with this unit and that charged this turn » the coordinated relative takes the head noun (`perimeter.charge_subjects`): the enemy charged, not us (Immortal Bulwark, Ferocious Sabrefangs, HORN TOSS, GRAPPLE-NET LAUNCHER…).
- **Set by the bearer's shooting**: « by this unit's shooting attacks », « SHOOT ability used by this unit », or a shooting-phase « attacks made with / targeted by this unit's <weapon> » → requirement `perimeter.SHOT_BY_BEARER` (« après tir du porteur ») on enemy-side effects, never on mortal damage (`realized.direct_damage` drops unknown requirements).
- **Enemy aura radius around the bearer**: a self/unknown scope with no range now reads « enemy units within 9\" of this unit », a reaction « for a unit within 12\" of this unit » and « Pick a visible unit within 12\" of the caster » (`extract.enemy_aura_range`) — `range_inches` only, scores unchanged (range only scores friendly auras and single targets).
- **Direct-damage readings** (found by the layer-3 direct-damage grid): retaliation (`perimeter.RETALIATION`, « riposte au contact ») and mortal damage on its own attacks (`perimeter.ON_OWN_ATTACKS`, « sur ses attaques ») label the enemy-side mortal damage; a condition heading the effect (« If this unit charged this turn, roll a dice… move 2D6"… Inflict D3 », Destructive Bulk; « If this unit is in combat, … », Carve a Path) now applies to all its effects, except a v34 one-time trigger that already carries it; « Allocate N damage points to the target » with a *friendly* declared target is a self-inflicted cost (Offering of Blood bonus → anecdotique, Atrocious Ministrations, Bellowing Waaagh!-Cries, Warpstone Innards); « If this unit would be automatically destroyed, it is not » (Avatar of Destruction, Entropy Endures) is a friendly protection, family `buff-perso-defensif`.
- **Drawbacks**: « this unit has STRIKE-FIRST but ward rolls cannot be made for it » (Elite Guardians) is a friendly `no_ward` worth 0; an enemy *bonus* (« add 1 to hit rolls for attacks made by that enemy unit », Dark Temptations) is family `autre`, not `debuff`; negated weapon abilities against a *friendly* target (Miasma of Pestilence, Weapon Curse) are `buff-donne`.
